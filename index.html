About Me

When I am from is a little more complicated as my childhood was evenly split across three metroplexes: Dallas, Austin, and Houston. I have resorted to just telling people 'I am a Texas gal at heart' rather than specifying one hometown.

Having grown up in many contexts, I aspire to think about the world analytically and openly. Many of my passions are rooted in exploring different city cultures, different experiences, and learning where I fit in best.

Outside of my academic and research work, I’m excited to be moving to North Carolina to pursue a career in online fraud detection—an area where I can combine analytical thinking with my interest in protecting and improving digital systems. I also have a deep love for politics, shaped by my time working on Capitol Hill, and I’m always energized by conversations about policy, institutions, and how communities function. At heart, I’m a walkable-city enthusiast who believes the best days start with good coffee and end with a long stroll. And as a proud gluten-free girlie, I’m constantly discovering new gluten-free restaurants and testing out new recipes. These interests ground me, give me joy, and inspire me to stay curious, connected, and open to new experiences.
